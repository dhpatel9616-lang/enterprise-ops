**Status: waiting for the owner's interview.** Nothing below is filled in yet,

## How to fill a section (the interview)

Ask the owner these questions one brand at a time, in plain words. Write
their answers into that brand's section, word for word where possible, and
open a PR so they can check it.

1. In one sentence, what does this brand do, and for whom?
2. Who is the ideal viewer/reader? (age range, job, what they're stuck on)
3. Pick 3 words for how it should sound (e.g. calm, blunt, playful).
4. Any words or phrases it should always use? Never use?
5. Colors and fonts, if any already exist (hex codes or a link to the logo).
6. Is there a recurring host character? Which one (see `character-bible`)?
7. How should posts sign off (name, handle, tagline)?
8. Topics that are off-limits or need extra care?
9. Two or three accounts or creators whose *feel* you like (for tone only;
   we never copy them).
10. Main revenue path and CTA for this brand (see `monetization`).

## Always true, for every brand

- Audience wording: "for beginners", never "for kids".
- Follow `content-rules` and `monetization`.
