The Sovereign's article promos. Realistic AI actors, so every appearance is
AI-labeled (`content-rules`). They are actors, never presented as real users,
reviewers, or employees. They must not resemble any real person.

All four look clearly 25+ on camera.

recognizable. Settings: apartments, a sports bar, a backyard, a car, a
kitchen, a gym.
