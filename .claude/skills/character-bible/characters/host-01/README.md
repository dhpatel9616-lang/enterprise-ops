# Host-01 (working name)

**Status:** style test. Name, personality details, and which brand it hosts
are for the owner to decide.

## Look
- Wide, pebble-shaped mint-green head with a darker chin shadow.
- A two-leaf sprout growing from the top of the head.
- Big oval eyes with dark pupils and a white highlight; short curved brows.
- Pink cheek patches with three small freckles each.
- Navy headset band, mustard ear-cup on the viewer's left, boom mic toward
  the mouth.
- Mustard hoodie with a navy front pocket and navy collar line; short rounded
  arms with mint hands.
- Thick dark-navy outlines (7 px head, 6 px elsewhere at 1000 px scale).

## Personality (draft for the owner to edit)
Friendly, curious, explains things simply for beginners. Never a customer,
never gives testimonials.

## Motion
Gentle bob (2.2 s cycle), slight head tilt, blinks about every 3 s, brows lift
while talking, right arm can wave. Mouth follows Rhubarb shapes A–H and X
(see `reference-sheet.png`).

## Don't
- Don't change colors or proportions without a PR.
- Don't add realistic textures; this character is flat-color cartoon only.
