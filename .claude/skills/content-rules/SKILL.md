# Content rules

## AI characters are actors
- Our AI/cartoon characters are clearly performers: hosts, narrators,
  explainers, sketch characters.
- They are **never** presented as real customers, users, clients, or
  experts, and never give testimonials, reviews, or "this worked for me"
  stories. (US FTC rules ban fake reviews and testimonials, including
  AI-generated ones.)
- Real results or quotes only come from real, named, consenting people, with
  the owner's written OK and proof on file.

## Realistic AI characters (PoolParty and Sovereign promos)
- Every video or image with a realistic AI character gets the platform's AI
  label **and** an on-screen "AI actor" or "Made with AI" note. No exceptions.
- They can demonstrate or explain (e.g. show PoolParty features, introduce a
  Sovereign issue). They never pretend to be a real user, employee, founder,
  journalist, or eyewitness, and never say "I use this and love it."
- For Sovereign (a media brand), an AI character never delivers news as if a
  human reporter were on the scene.

## Label realistic AI content
- If a viewer could mistake AI-made footage, voice, or images for something
  real (a real-looking person, place, or event), it must carry the
  platform's AI label **and** a short on-screen or caption note such as
  "Made with AI." Details per platform: `platform-formats`.
- Clearly cartoon content (like our SVG hosts) doesn't need the platform
  label, but never pretend it's hand-drawn by a person if it wasn't.
- If you're unsure whether something counts as realistic, label it.

## No real people's likenesses
- Don't generate, imitate, or edit in the face, body, or voice of any real
  person (celebrities, politicians, creators, private people), including the
  owner, unless the owner explicitly provides and approves their own.
- Don't make characters that resemble existing copyrighted characters or
  mascots.
- Don't use other creators' footage, music, or art without a licence.

## Claims
- No invented numbers, earnings claims, or guarantees. Money, security, and
  legal topics get "educational only, not advice" wording where relevant.
- Say "for beginners", never "for kids".

## When in doubt
Stop, leave the item in Notion with a note, and ask the owner.
