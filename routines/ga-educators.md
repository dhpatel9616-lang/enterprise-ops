# GA Educator Research (step of Builder + Leads, Wednesdays)

Goal (owner, 2026-11-05): get The Global Aggregate used in classrooms by
teachers and professors who teach news or media literacy, journalism, or
civics. Notion → Automation (Claude-managed) → **GA Educators**
(`collection://d38e90fe-764c-4aac-aee0-7bc24d3430e7`); view mode or fetch,
never SQL mode. Rows with Status **Ready** are emailed automatically
(Enterprise-leads `ingest/podcast-guests.js`, before Thursday's outreach run).

1. **Add up to 10 new people a week** as Wanted (skip anyone already in the
   table): web-search for faculty who teach news literacy, media literacy,
   journalism or civics; universities and community colleges in DC,
   Maryland, Virginia and Pennsylvania first, then anywhere in the US. High
   school teachers are fine when the school publishes their email.
   Librarians: no (owner). Fill School and Course or role from what you read.
2. **For up to 10 Wanted rows** (oldest first), set Researching, then:
   - **Contact:** the email on their official faculty, department or school
     page only. Contact email + the page in Contact source. **Never guess an
     address** (no firstname.lastname@ patterns) and never use data-broker
     or "find anyone's email" sites. If their school's site can't be
     reached from this environment, set "Needs owner" with the reason (the
     owner can allow `*.edu` in the environment's network settings).
     Nothing public: "No contact found".
   - **Personal line** (the email's first sentence, owner's voice, no
     flattery): one specific, true sentence about their course or work
     ("I saw your News Literacy course at Wake Forest teaches students to
     check where a story comes from").
   - Both filled: Status **Ready**.
3. Queued rows whose Raw Notes show a reply: set **Replied** and add a line to
   the Strategy Memo's owner to-do ("<name> replied about The Global
   Aggregate: answer in Gmail"). Never reply yourself.

Report in the memo: rows added, Ready, Needs owner, No contact found, replies.
